How to sketch your floor plan

Follow these simple steps to draw a floor plan that Spacify's AI can read accurately.

1

Draw the outer boundary

Draw a rectangle or L-shape representing the total plot area. Use thick lines for outer walls. Keep it proportional — a 40×30 ft plot should look wider than it is tall.

2

Divide into rooms

Draw internal walls as lines dividing the space. Each enclosed area = one room. Don't worry about perfect straight lines — the AI handles rough sketches well.

3

Label every room

Write a label inside each room. You can use full names or short codes:

MB → Master Bedroom
LR → Living Room
K → Kitchen
B1, B2 → Bedrooms
Bath → Bathroom
WIC → Walk-in Closet
PR → Pooja Room
St → Study
Bal → Balcony
Store → Store Room
Stair → Staircase
DR → Dining Room
4

Add dimensions (optional)

Write room sizes next to labels like MB 14×13. If you skip this, the AI will estimate based on proportions.

5

Take a clear photo

Place the sketch on a flat surface. Good lighting, no shadows across the drawing. Hold the camera directly above (bird's eye view). The whole sketch should be visible with no cropping.

Tips for best results

  • →Use a dark pen on white paper for maximum contrast
  • →Keep labels inside the room boundary
  • →One room per enclosed area — don't overlap
  • →For multi-floor homes, draw each floor separately and note Floor 1, Floor 2
  • →Digital sketches (iPad, tablet) work even better than paper
